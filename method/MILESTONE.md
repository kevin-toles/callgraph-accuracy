## What remains after the milestone

- **Open defects** in CAPABILITIES.md. Each has a failing golden case ready as its RED test, so a fix is one TDD cycle.
- **Undecided questions** in GOLDEN_AUTHOR_QUESTIONS.md. Each is decided only under change control (a ruling, then a fixture), never by a repo's score.
